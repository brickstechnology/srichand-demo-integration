# Company and brand facts (collected 18 Sep 2026)

## Company
- บริษัท ศรีจันทร์สหโอสถ จำกัด (Srichand United Dispensary Co., Ltd.), a Thai company.
- Founded in 1948 by Phong Hanutsaha; incorporated in 1988; rebranded in 2014 under third-generation CEO Rawit Hanutsaha (รวิศ หาญอุตสาหะ).
- The Original Powder recipe dates from 1948.

## Brands
- SRICHAND — the heritage brand.
- SASI (ศศิ) — launched in 2017 for roughly ages 16–35, at value prices. Same company.
- SRICHAND Baby — baby powders.

## Presenters
- BamBam — SRICHAND IN-SKIN skincare since December 2024, now fronting Phyto Camellia PDRN; also the BamBam × SRICHAND lip balms.
- Zee and NuNew — Sunlution sunscreen (Thailand and Laos, from April 2026); the "First Step" sets.
- Baifern — base makeup.
- Kao — SASI.

## Where to buy
- Online: srichand.com; official stores on Shopee, Lazada and TikTok.
- Retail: 7-Eleven, Watsons, Boots, CJ More, Lotus's, Big C, Tops, Konvy, Eveandboy, Beautrium, Multy.
- Export markets: Japan, Laos, China, the Philippines.
Prices and promotions in stores and marketplaces can differ from srichand.com; this assistant only knows srichand.com prices.
