## SASI lines
Acne Sol (blemish-prone skin: cleanser, toner pad, sunscreen, cushion, concealer, powders) · Girls Can · Magic Matte · Sugar Rush · Perfect Personal Color · Mojo Bar · All Day Fix · Tomato Tone Up · lip tints, balms and brow pencils.

## What is worth explaining to a customer
- Sunlution uses modern photostable filters (Tinosorb S, Uvinul A Plus, Uvinul T150). "SPF50+ PA++++" on the box does not tell that story.
- Phyto Camellia discloses its percentages, and several lines publish their test protocols (72-hour hydration; irritation tests on sensitive skin).
- Two routes into anti-ageing: a tolerance-first retinoid (Resurface) and a retinoid-free alternative (Timeless).
- One company, two price ladders: SASI Acne Sol serves a student budget without leaving the family.
