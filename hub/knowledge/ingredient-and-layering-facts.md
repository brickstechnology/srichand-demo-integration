# Ingredient, layering and usage facts

Internal reasoning notes. Anything said to a customer must be re-worded with the claim-wording rules.

## Layering
| Combination | Rule |
|---|---|
| Retinoid with an exfoliating acid (AHA or BHA) | Different nights until skin is fully used to both |
| AHA with BHA | One exfoliant per session |
| HPR retinoid with bakuchiol | Fine to alternate; start one, add the other after 4 weeks |
| Any retinoid or exfoliant | Daily SPF50+ PA++++ is essential |
| Vitamin C derivatives under sunscreen | Good pairing — mornings |
| Retinoid with ceramides, ectoin, panthenol, cica | Good pairing — buffers dryness |
| Niacinamide, tranexamic acid, vitamin C derivatives | Combine freely |
Thin to thick; one new active at a time; two weeks between introductions.

## Starting the retinoid (Resurface Pro-Retinol Intense Serum)
Weeks 1–2: two nights a week. Weeks 3–6: every other night. Then nightly if comfortable. A pea-sized amount on dry skin, avoiding eye corners and lips, moisturiser on top. Mild dryness early on is expected; stinging or peeling means stop and return to barrier care. Not during pregnancy or nursing — the bakuchiol serum (Timeless) is the retinoid-free alternative, still with a doctor's confirmation.

## Special groups
| Group | Guidance |
|---|---|
| Sensitive or reactive | Barrier Boost serum and gel cream, Phyto Camellia cleanser, Sunlution Rosia or Moisture Burst Watery. Nothing new for two weeks. Avoid Super C Intense Serum, Original Powder Mask, tea-tree products, toner pads. |
| Oily in a humid climate | Light layers, a matte sunscreen, Translucent Powder for the afternoon. Oily skin still needs water; skipping moisturiser often increases shine. |
| Teens | Three steps at most: gentle cleanser, light moisturiser if needed, sunscreen. Toner pad two nights a week only if tolerated. Persistent acne: a parent plus a pharmacist or dermatologist. |
| Pregnant or nursing | No retinoids. Every other active: "confirm with your doctor". |
| After shaving | Fragrance-free, alcohol-free soothing serum; no alcohol aftershave; fragranced products often sting. |
| Outdoor sport, swimming | A sunscreen whose official copy claims water resistance; reapply after heavy sweating. |
