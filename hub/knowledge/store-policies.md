# Store policies (srichand.com, read 18 Sep 2026 — re-verify before launch)

## Shipping
- Free shipping from 599 baht (after discounts). Below that: 50 baht flat.
- Carriers and delivery times are not published. As a guide only: Bangkok and nearby 1–2 days, other provinces 2–4 days after dispatch.

## Payment
- Accepted: PromptPay QR, credit or debit card, mobile banking, TrueMoney.
- Not offered: cash on delivery, instalments.
- A PromptPay QR or mobile-banking payment must be completed within 30 minutes. After that the order can no longer be paid: it is cancelled, the items return to the cart, and any points or coupon used are given back.

## Order statuses
| Status | Meaning for the customer |
|---|---|
| pending_payment | Order placed, waiting for payment |
| paid | Payment received |
| packing | Being packed |
| shipped | With the courier; a tracking number exists |
| delivered | Courier reports delivery |
| cancelled | Cancelled (unpaid in time, or by staff) |
| refunded | Money returned |
Courier stages: label created → picked up → in transit → out for delivery → delivered (or failed attempt, returned).

## Returns, refunds, exchanges, cancellation
- Report a problem within 3 days of delivery.
- Only wrong or defective items. No change-of-mind returns or exchanges.
- An unboxing video is requested as evidence.
- Goods are sent back sealed within 14 days; refunds can take up to 30 business days.
- An order can be cancelled only before it reaches "Ready to Ship".
- Staff decide every claim.
