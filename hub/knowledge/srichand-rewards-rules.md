# Srichand Rewards (official rules unless marked)

## The programme
- One level for everyone. No tiers are published.
- Earning: every 25 baht of net spend (after discounts, not counting shipping) earns 1 point. Amounts under 25 baht on a receipt earn nothing.
- Points last one year, "counted from the year received" (the exact rule is ambiguous). An account inactive for three years may be closed.
- Points cannot be exchanged for cash or transferred.
- No birthday or welcome benefit is published.

## When points arrive
- srichand.com, Shopee and Lazada orders: within 17 days, once the account is linked.
- Physical-store receipts uploaded in LINE: within 48 hours.
- TikTok Shop is not mentioned in the terms.
- Missing points must be reported within 30 days.
- In this prototype, points are added the moment an order is paid.

## Using points
- Srichand publishes no value for a point ("at the company's discretion") and no reward list.
- PROTOTYPE ASSUMPTION: 1 point = 0.50 baht off an order, and a small sample reward list. Present these as what the demo system offers, never as official Srichand terms.

## Joining
- Official flow today: add LINE @srichand1948 (SASI: @sasidiary), register with a Thai mobile number and SMS OTP, then link the srichand.com account using the member ID and latest order number.
- In this prototype the assistant can sign a new member up in the chat: first name, Thai mobile number, birth date, explicit acceptance of the terms and privacy notice, and a separate yes or no for promotions. One account per mobile number. No SMS code is sent in the prototype, so the number is stored as unverified.
- Minimum age 16; ages 16–19 need a guardian to confirm. (The published terms say 20+, the LINE form says 16+ — Srichand needs to settle this.)
- Member numbers look like SCR0260041.
- A chat is linked to a member by staff. Until then, points and orders cannot be shown in that chat.
