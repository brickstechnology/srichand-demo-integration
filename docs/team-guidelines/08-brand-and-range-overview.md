## The story worth telling (all verifiable in the database)
1. **Sunlution's filter system is genuinely modern.** Acne Care combines Tinosorb S, Uvinul A Plus and Uvinul T150 with avobenzone and titanium dioxide — photostable, broad-spectrum filters. Two of them (Uvinul A Plus / DHHB and Uvinul T150 / ethylhexyl triazone) are still **not permitted in US sunscreens**, and the third (Tinosorb S / bemotrizinol) was only cleared by the US FDA in **June 2026**. "SPF50+ PA++++" on the box tells a customer none of this; a personalised explanation can.
2. **Disclosed percentages** (Phyto Camellia: PDRN 0.5%, niacinamide 5%, arbutin 0.1%) and **named test protocols** (72-hour hydration, Dermscan; irritation tests on sensitive skin, n=32) are credibility assets the advisor can quote exactly.
3. **A tolerance-first retinoid** (Resurface: HPR with a full ceramide / cholesterol / phytosphingosine complex) and a **retinoid-free alternative** (Timeless: bakuchiol) give the advisor two honest routes into anti-ageing.
4. **One company, two price ladders.** SASI's Acne Sol line lets the advisor serve a 17-year-old on a ฿150 budget without leaving the family.
5. **Where the range stops** is just as clear — see guideline 04. The biggest gap sits directly on the brand's oil-control heritage.
